## [0.1.0] — 2026-10-05

### Added

- `fuzz`: seeded campaign runner (seed `base_seed + cycle`) that shrinks every failure
  and records it as a `MinimizedFailure` with its seed; `FuzzReport` with `is_clean`.
- `fuzz_stateful`: state-transition-aware campaigns generated by walking an
  `AbstractModel`.
- `replay_failure` to re-run a stored failure, and optional on-disk failure storage
  (`failures/<digest>.json`, deduplicated by content).
- `Corpus`: directory-backed, content-addressed (SHA-256) corpus with `open`, `add`,
  `entries`, `entries_json`, `len` and `is_empty`; deterministic entry ordering.
- `input_digest` and `sequence_to_json` helpers.
